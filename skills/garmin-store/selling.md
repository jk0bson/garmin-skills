# Selling

From forums, press and the SDK, October 2026. Not official Garmin documentation: verify on your own merchant dashboard.

- Merchant account (Merchant Onboarding, via Adyen): about 100 USD per year, plus 15 % of the price before tax. Garmin adds taxes and pays card fees; currency conversion and digital taxes are deducted from payouts.
- Payout on the 1st of the month, minimum 10 USD or 10 €. Individuals accepted (ID, phone, address), 18 or older.
- Prices come in fixed tiers. Refunds possible within 2 days.
- Sales only reach recent watches (fēnix 7/8, Forerunner 165/255/265/955/965/970, vívoactive 5/6, Venu 3/4/Sq 2, recent Instinct, Edge). Older models such as Forerunner 245 and fēnix 6 cannot buy.
- "Payment Required" badge when the main function is paid; always for a watch face.
- Trials (`isTrial`, unlock keys) exist for apps, not watch faces.
- A free-to-paid switch pulls the app for review and makes existing users pay (promo codes possible).
- "1+ Downloads" also counts free installs, including your own: confirm real sales in the merchant reports.
- Most downloaded in a January 2026 third-party ranking: music, health, then running data fields (power, maps, wind, battery). Few watch faces near the top.
